
## Goal

Rebrand "Creative Blockchain" → **Creative AI**. Pivot every page, every primitive, and all 1,000 mega-prompts from Sepolia/MetaMask/Privy/Pinata/Etherscan to **ElevenLabs**. Keep the editorial gold/cream folio look exactly as-is, keep the 10 disciplines × 100 entries structure, keep the AISA generator pipeline. Replace the Five-Secret Protocol with the **One-Key Protocol** (just `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`).

## The four new "kernels"

Replace `src/data/ideas/hooks.json` (currently Sepolia / IPFS / Privy / NFT) with:

1. **tts-narration** — Text-to-Speech (`openai/v1/audio/speech`-style proxy, streaming PCM, 11+ voices).
2. **voice-agent** — Conversational AI via `@elevenlabs/react` `useConversation`, WebRTC, client tools.
3. **realtime-stt** — Live transcription via `useScribe` + `scribe_v2_realtime`.
4. **music-sfx** — Generative music tracks + sound effects (`/v1/music`, `/v1/sound-generation`).

Each idea's existing `quantumHookId` / `chainHookId` maps deterministically: NFT-provenance/IPFS ideas → `music-sfx`, social/identity ideas → `voice-agent`, logging/text ideas → `realtime-stt`, default → `tts-narration`. AISA regeneration then refines copy per discipline.
